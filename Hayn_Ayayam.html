A Cellular Quest

Cell Division & Cell Cycle Board Game

📖 Instructions

Objective

Learn about cell division, cell cycle, and cell organelles by being the first player to reach tile 100!

How to Play

  • Players take turns rolling the dice to move forward
  • Answer questions correctly to earn power-up cards
  • Use power-up cards strategically to gain advantages

Special Tiles

  • 🧬 DNA Ladder: Climb up to skip ahead
  • 💀 Apoptosis (Virus): Move down one tile (e.g., 15→6)
  • ⚡ Power Question: Answer correctly to get a random power-up card
  • 🚩 Checkpoint: Wrong answer = move 3 steps back
  • G1, S, G2, M Phases: Answer correctly = move 1 tile forward

Power-Up Cards

  • 🧠 Wild Card: Use as any power-up
  • ⚡ Energy Up: Move 3 steps forward
  • 🛡️ Protect: Block next apoptosis
  • 🔄 Move (ER): Roll + 3 steps
  • 📦 Transport: Move 10 tiles up
  • 🍽️ Digest: Opponent moves 3 back
  • 🌿 Go Green: Jump to 3rd green tile
  • 🧬 Inherit: Move to opponent's position
  • 💧 Cytoplasm: Swap with opponent
